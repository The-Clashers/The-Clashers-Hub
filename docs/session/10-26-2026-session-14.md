## Decisions

- Dropdowns replace the filter chips. Session 12 chose chips for the closed option sets, the 44 px touch target, and zero new dependencies; those reasons still hold, but four chip rows ate the first screenful and the owner asked for dropdowns. The reversal keeps the parts worth keeping: no new dependency (the control is built on RN primitives, CON-04 intact), the 44 px floor, and the apply-on-submit rule with its "changed since the last search" hint, so the tier's cache-key economy is untouched.
- The control follows the WAI-ARIA select-only combobox pattern: a role=button trigger with aria-haspopup="listbox" and aria-expanded, a role=listbox of role=option rows with aria-selected. react-native-web forwards all of those props to the DOM, and its PressResponder gives the trigger and the options Enter/Space activation for free. Options carry tabIndex=-1 so the tab order stays trigger-then-out; arrows move focus inside instead.
- The open list renders through a portal at document.body with position:fixed. react-native-web gives every View position:relative + zIndex:0, so each View is its own stacking context and a z-index inside the search panel can never climb over the tag panel or the results below it; the first attempt painted the options under the tag input (caught in the browser, not in review). The portal sidesteps every stacking context at once. The app page never scrolls (the FlatList owns scrolling, the filter row sits above it), so the captured trigger coordinates stay valid while open; a window resize closes the list.
- Keyboard contract, verified in the browser: Enter or Space opens, ArrowDown/ArrowUp on a closed trigger opens too (the native select behavior), arrows walk the options, Home/End jump, Enter or Space picks the focused option, Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger, Tab lets focus move and closes, a real press outside closes.
- The option rows show no hover highlight on purpose: pressed and selected states carry the feedback, RN 0.88's Pressable types carry no mouse events, and the design language's "data tools answer questions, they don't perform" is cheaper to honor than to fight the types for.
- The results count pluralizes. "1 clans" was on the screen whenever a search matched exactly one clan; now it reads "1 clan".
- The tag-box counter only appears within ten characters of the 21-character ceiling, as "N left". A bare always-on "21" read as noise, and real tags are 3 to 9 characters.
- The session 13 review items: `showError` now keys off `touched` alone so every reason surfaces after a rejected submit, empty included. Session 12's dev-log hygiene rule (no PR URLs) is restored by dropping the line from the session 13 log.
