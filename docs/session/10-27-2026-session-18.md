## Decisions

- The split ran on the session 17 branch because the standards and the first payment belong to one review: the reviewer reads the rule and the code that follows it in the same PR.
- Promotion followed STD-15 exactly: everything the 07 section 4 inventory names moved out, and the two screen-specific fragments that remain (the error copy mapping, the list footer) stay in the screen file with their own styles, per STD-16.
- `warFrequencyLabel` moved to `lib/` rather than living in the card component because two layers need it: the screen's dropdown options and the card's stat rendering. One table, two consumers, no drift.
- The pill padding and stretch differences were unified rather than parameterized. A button API with three padding knobs would be its own smell; two imperceptible presentational deltas were accepted and recorded here instead.
- HelloClanScreen and TokenScreen still carry the threading pattern on purpose: per the paydown policy their cleanup rides the next card that touches each file, and this card did not touch them.
