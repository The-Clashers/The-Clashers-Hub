## Decisions

- `leagueTier` over `league`: the live payload was checked first, and the
  legacy field reads as history after the trophy reset (Legend League on a
  280-trophy member) while the tier matches what the game shows. The tier's
  rank number rides inside the name string ("Electro League 31"), so no
  separate rank field is modeled; when the game splits them, the parser
  grows one field.
- The bare tag is canonicalized back to `#TAG` at the screen, not inside
  the fetch: the cache key, the error copy, and the on-screen tag all use
  the canonical form, and the tier request keeps its `%23` encoding exactly
  where it was. Old links resolving matters more than URL aesthetics
  uniformity.
- The ratio boxes disable rather than hide while needs-attention is off,
  and they are the only writer of the target: mounted-drafts-never-reset
  beats re-deriving a displayable pair from a float.
- The tab strip is the recorded exception to the 44 px floor rather than a
  silent deviation: the owner asked for the height cut, the doc says so,
  and focus visibility keeps the keyboard path honest.
- Page flow beats virtualization for the roster: 50 rows is the hard cap,
  the search results and the progression grid are the lists that grow, and
  a viewport-boxed roster was actively lying to phone visitors about how
  much data there was.
- The chip shows `ratio : 1` rather than raw counters because Donations and
  Received are already columns; a chip that repeats them makes the row say
  the same thing twice (05 section 2 updated to match).
