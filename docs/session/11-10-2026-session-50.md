## Decisions

- Diagnosed that index.html configured height: 100% on #root and body, which constrained the container strictly to viewport height and forced the footer to render at the bottom of the viewport over overflowing screen cards.
- Changed html, body, and #root to min-height: 100%, allowing the root flex column to grow naturally with page content so the footer renders below all sections in normal document flow.
- Added flexShrink: 0 to both appRoot and footer so neither flex item compresses when content is tall, keeping the footer at the bottom of short pages via marginTop: auto and at the bottom of the document on long pages.
