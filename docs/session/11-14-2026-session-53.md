## Decisions

- The metric set is 05 section 4's list read literally: Town Hall and XP level under Account; rushed overall plus the categories (lower-better); the union of both sides' home-village heroes, a hero one side has not unlocked rendering against the dash; lifetime war stars and season attack wins under War record; and the key achievements as curated data, the game's lifetime counters. `War Hero` is deliberately absent from that curation because its value is the payload's own warStars, which the war record already compares once.
- Direction coloring is the semantic pair the doc names (better/worse): the leading value in success ink, the trailing in danger, a tie in primary, an incomparable row quiet. The delta is the leader's margin, unsigned, in the leader's own green. The first implementation rendered the delta signed toward A and colored by its sign, and browser verification caught it contradicting the value colors on the lower-is-better rushed rows (a "+7.2" green delta on a row B was winning, because B's smaller percentage is the bigger raw number); the redesign gives every row one signal, and the tests follow it.
- The compare engine imports neither the rushed engine's internals nor the API client: the screen-side adapter trues each payload plus her rushed verdict into structural sides, the same adapter pattern the rushed engine itself uses, so the section 5 boundary stays honest by construction.
- One query per side, keyed `['player', tag]` exactly like the player detail, so a visitor who just opened either player answers from the shared cache and the two TTL layers decay together. A failed side renders one error panel naming that side's tag with the contract's own copy; the comparison never renders half-loaded.
- The identity cards ground the numbers without verdicts (Town Hall, XP, league) and the whole card links to the player's full profile, the roster rows' own tap-through. The deliberate limitation rides as quiet copy under the subtitle so the absence of an overall winner banner reads as a decision, not an omission.
- The compare landing already navigated to `/compare/players`; the route it needed is what landed here. The landing's Compare Clans mode still routes to `/compare/clans`, which stays unbuilt until T-M3-4 (the state session 43 left it in, unchanged).
- Recent searches are not recorded by the compare screen: the lookup flows that own recording (the tag box, the player detail) keep it; the compare is its own feature and the landing did not record either.
